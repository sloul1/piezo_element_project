Best practice

Create a master template in PowerPoint, Figma, Illustrator, Inkscape, or Visio and duplicate it for every new figure. Keep:

identical title position
identical section header position
identical footer position
identical color mapping
identical font hierarchy

This will make the whole publication look like a single professionally designed figure set rather than independently created graphics.
